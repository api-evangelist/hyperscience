# Hyperscience

> Hyperscience is the market leader in intelligent document processing and a provider of enterprise AI infrastructure software. The Hyperscience Hypercell platform unlocks the value of an organization's back office data through the automation of end-to-end processes, and transforms complex documents into LLM and RAG-ready data to power new enterprise GenAI experiences. This enables organizations to transform manual, siloed processes into a strategic advantage, resulting in a faster path to decisions, actions, and revenue; positive and engaging customer, public, and patient experiences; and dramatic increases in productivity.


## Use Cases

- Intelligent Document Processing & Data Extraction: Hyperscience automates the intelligent document processing of structured, semi-structured, and unstructured documents, accurately extracting text, handwriting, barcodes, and more-even from varied layouts and formats. Advanced pre- and post-processing with proprietary machine learning (ML) and vision-language models (VLM) ensures over 98% accuracy and high-quality, LLM-ready data.
- Generative AI & LLM Applications: Hyperscience integrates Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI (GenAI) into workflows to transform documents into RAG-ready (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) data for advanced search, chat, and analysis. It supports contextual understanding, summarization, translation, and content generation while validating and enhancing LLM outputs for reliable decision-making.
- Process Automation & Workflow Orchestration: Hyperscience automates sorting, routing, and data entry with modular workflows that integrate classification, transcription, APIs, and custom logic. Human-in-the-loop (HITL) capabilities ensure up to 100% field-level completeness, while automated processes deliver up to 99.5% accuracy at scale.
- Model Management & Continuous Improvement: Hyperscience offers a full ModelOps framework for training, deploying, monitoring, and improving AI models with minimal data (40-400 documents) and expertise required. Built-in AI co-pilots handle anomaly detection, data labeling, and drift management to ensure accuracy and adaptability over time.
- Data Security & Compliance: Hyperscience safeguards sensitive data with encryption, redaction, robust role-based access control (RBAC), and detailed audit logs, meeting strict industry and government standards. Certifications include SOC 2 Type 2, Cyber Essentials Plus, GDPR/UK GDPR, CPRA, and FedRAMP High for U.S. public sector readiness.

## Deployment Options

- SaaS Offering (Software-as-a-Service): Hyperscience's SaaS runs on AWS with global data residency options, Azure marketplace, Google Cloud Platform Marketplace, and FedRAMP High authorization for secure, regulated use. The multi-tenant Kubernetes architecture supports private tenancy, autoscaling, and GPU-enabled model training for high-performance processing.
- On-Premise: Hyperscience can be deployed in customer-managed data centers with full control over infrastructure, supporting Linux (Ubuntu, RHEL), Docker/Podman, and optional Kubernetes orchestration. It operates in connected or air-gapped environments, scales horizontally, and integrates with major databases, file stores, and external authentication systems.
- Private Cloud: Similar to on-premise deployment, but run on a virtual private cloud controlled by the customer. Private cloud offers the benefit of hosting Vision Language Models (VLMs) and Large Language Models (LLMs) within the client's private environment for enhanced security and control.
- Hybrid Cloud: Hyperscience is designed to be flexible and adaptable, supporting hybrid cloud models that combine aspects of on-premise and public/private cloud deployments to meet specific customer requirements.
